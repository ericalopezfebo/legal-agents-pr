# Puerto Rico lawyer-craft sources

This file records public practice materials used only to discover lawyer-craft capabilities for the methodology layer. They are **not legal authority** and must not be copied, reproduced, or substantially paraphrased into agent prompts. Any procedural, evidentiary, ethical, or substantive proposition suggested by them must be independently verified against primary law.

## Depositions and witness preparation

- Microjuris al Día — *¿Cómo me preparo para una deposición?* (2014; page later updated). Useful at a high level for witness orientation, truthful preparation, listening, avoiding speculation, handling ambiguous questions, fatigue, and understanding the deposition process.
- Microjuris al Día — *Abogados comparten sus impresiones sobre cómo deponer adecuadamente* (2018). Discovery source for practical deposition habits and professional judgment.
- Microjuris al Día — *Cómo prepararse para la deposición del perito* (2014). Discovery source for expert-deposition preparation and defining objectives before questioning.
- Microjuris al Día — *Cómo calmar la ansiedad de un cliente ante un juicio o deposición* (2018). Discovery source for ethical client/witness preparation and process familiarization.
- Microjuris al Día — *Breve análisis comparativo de la normativa estatal y federal referente a la prueba pericial* (2021). Use only to identify issues requiring verification in the Puerto Rico and federal evidence rules and controlling precedent.

## Cross-examination and trial craft

- Microjuris al Día — *El ABC del contrainterrogatorio* (2024). Discovery source for chapter-based cross-examination, precise objectives, simple leading questions, listening, avoiding unnecessary questions, witness control, and identifying door-opening risk. Authorities mentioned in the article must be verified independently before legal use.
- Microjuris — *6 consejos para mejorar las destrezas de litigio*. Discovery source for deliberate practice and courtroom-skill development.
- Microjuris al Día — *Consejos para el nuevo litigante: Cómo prepararte para tu primera vista ante el Tribunal* (2019). Discovery source for hearing objectives, file and document preparation, oral outlines, direct answers to judicial questions, forum familiarity, and post-hearing self-review.
- Microjuris — *7 consejos para perfeccionar tu argumentación en una videoconferencia*. Discovery source for remote oral-advocacy preparation; official court orders and procedural rules control.

## Legal research, writing, negotiation, and professional workflow

- Microjuris — *7 consejos para una investigación legal de último minuto en una revista jurídica*. Discovery source for triaging research under time pressure. Secondary research is never a substitute for verifying primary authority.
- Microjuris al Día — *Cómo mejorar mi redacción jurídica* (2023). Discovery source for clarity, organization, audience awareness, and editing discipline.
- Microjuris — *6 consejos sobre las alegaciones preacordadas*. Discovery source only for negotiation and client-counsel workflow ideas; criminal procedure, ethics, voluntariness requirements, and judicial approval must come from verified law.
- Microjuris al Día — *5 consejos esenciales para que los abogados no pierdan una fecha límite* (2017). Discovery source for redundancy, calendaring discipline, and deadline-control workflows. The actual triggering event, computation rule, jurisdictional effect, and applicable deadline must be verified independently.

## Implementation rule

Extract only high-level capabilities and controls from these materials. Rewrite those capabilities from first principles inside the relevant craft agent. Do not preserve source wording, source sequence, source examples, or proprietary checklists. Legal validity always comes from the substantive/evidence/procedure agents and verified primary authority.
